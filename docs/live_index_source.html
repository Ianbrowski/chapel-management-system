    Our Faith & Mission

    Who we are, what we believe, and how we serve our community.

    Our Vision

    "The Iglesia Filipina Independiente envisions a renewed church that is truly Filipino, independent, and responsive to the needs of the people. We are committed to proclaiming the Gospel, promoting justice, peace, and human dignity."

    Our Mission
    • Proclaim the Good News of God's Kingdom through worship, teaching, and service.
    • Administer the sacraments and nurture the spiritual life of our members.
    • Promote social justice, human dignity, and selfless care for our neighbors.
    • Uphold Filipino identity and heritage in reverent Christian fellowship.

    Pro Deo et Patria • For God and Country

    Sharing Christ's love, caring for our neighbors, and serving our community with compassion.

    Faith Justice Peace Fellowship

    About Our Church

    A community of faith, freedom, and fellowship rooted in the history of the Filipino people.

    Our History

    The Iglesia Filipina Independiente Cathedral stands as a testament to faith, resilience, and the Filipino spirit of independence. Our cathedral has been a beacon of hope and spiritual guidance for generations.

    1902
    Foundation

    The Iglesia Filipina Independiente was founded, establishing our autonomous Filipino church.

    1925
    Cathedral Built

    Construction of our magnificent cathedral was completed, becoming a landmark of faith.

    1975
    Major Restoration

    Extensive restoration work preserved the cathedral's historic beauty for future generations.

    2020
    Digital Transformation

    Launched modern management systems to better serve our growing parish community.
